Modelling notes

Vacancy is calculated from lease dates, not separately entered. Rent-free starts at new lease commencement. Reviews are modelled as a specified annual rent from a specified date. Exit capitalisation uses the rent applicable at exit; this is a simplified valuation, not a term-and-reversion valuation. Break rights are flagged as risks, not assumed to be exercised.

What the figures mean

IRR is the effective annualised return on the monthly cash flows. NPV is discounted at the fund's unlevered hurdle. A negative NPV indicates a shortfall against that hurdle, not necessarily an absolute cash loss. Debt is modelled as interest-only with an opening draw, financing fees and repayment at exit. Tax, purchaser costs, covenant breaches and break exercise probabilities are not modelled. Review and exit valuations are assumptions, not market evidence.
